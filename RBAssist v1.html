The DJ library problem

Your library is more than filenames and tags.

A real DJ library is years of downloads, edits, duplicates, missing paths, half-tagged tracks, Rekordbox exports, Bandcamp CSVs, test crates, forgotten gems, and broken references. Rekordbox is built for performance prep. rbassist is the intelligence layer around it.

What rbassist does

A local AI workbench for crates, tags, search, and repair.

Five workflows, one local app. Each one reads your real folders, your real Rekordbox XML, and your real listening history — not a sanitized cloud version of them.

01
SEED

Find tracks from a seed

Pick a track and get ranked recommendations from local audio embeddings and DJ-aware scoring.

02
VIBE

Describe a vibe

Search by plain English — “dark heavy dubstep,” “rolling bass pressure” — against semantic indexes.

03
EXPAND

Expand a playlist

Start from a Rekordbox playlist and grow it with similarity, diversity, and key/tempo controls.

04
REPAIR

Repair the library

Audit missing paths, stale metadata, duplicate candidates, embedding gaps, and Rekordbox references.

05
LAB

Compare model heads

Use Models Lab to compare MERT, CLAP, MuQ, and MuLan side-by-side before promoting them.

Live on local GPU — no upload, no API key required HNSW · SQLite ModelHeadCatalog · NiceGUI + Typer CLI
Multi-model intelligence

Different models hear different things. rbassist keeps them honest.

rbassist does not collapse every model into one fake universal score. It keeps separate indexes for different model families, tracks coverage in a catalog, and compares outputs before promoting them into DJ-facing workflows.

Local-first safety

Built around reviewable local workflows.

rbassist is designed for the way DJs actually protect their libraries: dry-run first, inspect the report, then apply only when the output makes sense. No silent mutations. No cloud uploads. No surprise deletes.

  • 01
    Local metadata and model sidecarsEmbeddings, indexes and analysis live next to your files, on your disk.
  • 02
    Dry-run-first repair workflowsEvery destructive operation produces a report before it touches a single tag or path.
  • 03
    Backup-first metadata mutationTag and Rekordbox writes are backed up to a timestamped sidecar before they apply.
  • 04
    Rekordbox treated as a secondary truth sourcerbassist reconciles, never overwrites Rekordbox without explicit operator approval.
  • 05
    Duplicate suppression overlay, not deletionSuspected duplicates are hidden and reviewable — never auto-deleted from disk.
  • 06
    Full-library rebuilds gatedReindex operations on the whole library require an explicit confirm and run under a supervised runner.
